Welcome! I am an incoming Research Associate at New York University Abu Dhabi (NYUAD). I hold a PhD ([ESRC-funded](https://www.ninedtp.ac.uk/wangyin-zhao-a-disaster-for-whom-the-conditional-impact-of-natural-disasters-on-civil-conflicts/)) from Durham University, and was a Doctoral Fellow at [Empirical Studies of Conflict (ESOC)](https://esoc.princeton.edu) at Princeton University (Sep–Dec 2025).

My research sits at the intersection of political violence, natural disasters, and resource governance. My PhD thesis, "Natural Disasters and Conflict Dynamics in Civil War", examines the conflict consequences of natural disasters — asking when and how natural disasters affect conflict dynamics in ongoing civil wars. I argue that disaster effects on conflict depend on the spatial configuration of territorial control, the presence of extractive companies, and how armed groups anticipate future power shifts. My work combines satellite-derived environmental data with geo-coded conflict records and causal inference methods. My research can be found in [*Journal of Conflict Resolution*](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00220027251346912).

I received an [MSc in International Relations (Research)](https://www.lse.ac.uk/international-relations) at LSE and a [BA in International Studies](https://www.nottingham.edu.cn/en/humanities-and-social-sciences/schools-and-department/international-studies/home.aspx) at University of Nottingham, Ningbo campus.
